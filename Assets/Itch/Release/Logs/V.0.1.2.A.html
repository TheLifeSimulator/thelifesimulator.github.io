Version 0.1.2.A

Minor bug fixes, performance enhancements, & UI tweaks.

Additions

  • Physics now scale dynamically to account for computational performance.
  • Metrics, statistics, and settings have been expanded to provide more oversight.
  • Implemented performance-based population culling and limitations.
  • Viral species are now tracked by their genetic background.

Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where gameplay mechanics wouldn't sleep on pause.
  • The cell inspector now opens automatically on mobile layouts.
  • Individual cell genetic controls now scale appropriately on mobile.
